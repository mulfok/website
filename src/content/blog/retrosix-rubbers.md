# retrosix snes rubbers
based in the UK, RetroSix offers a lot of replacement retro parts, or building services. living in Scotland, this is pretty appealing.

note that my use case is pretty much exclusively speedrunning super metroid, so your mileage may vary on other games.

## d-pad
phew! the d-pad is quite stiff! you can definitely feel when it clicks down and touches the board. it's not a bad feeling if you like a lot of feedback.

as a nice bonus, it's quite difficult to accidentally up/down when you don't want to, and I rarely break spin when wall jumping.

notably, downbacks are really tough on this dpad. they're hard to start, and seemingly impossible to maintain, unless you use more force than normal. in the SN30 though, it behaves much more like I expect it to. so if you have a third party PCB or such it will probably be fine(?)

## l/r
these seem like way too much trouble.

they feel quite firm, which again is not a bad feeling if you like some feedback.

In my OEM controller, they perform very differently every time you open the controller. it's usually the fault of the controller, and other rubbers are guilty of this, but not to the same extent as these.

they seem to register intermittently, and you have to use force. if you press lightly they won't register at all.in my SN30, they're fine, they just feel firm. though I attribute the better performance to the newer board and likely better contact surfaces of the controller.

looking inside, when you press down on the button, they don't depress fully. more like one side of the dome will go down and the other will stay up. it's not as obvious (but still there a little) in OEM, but on the SN30 it's very noticeable. I bought five sets of rubbers, and they all seem to be like this.

I won't write off the L/R rubbers completely, but they don't seem exceptional.

## start/select
they work good, nothing crazy. They have a decent travel distance, and when fully pressed it is flush with the shell.

the rubbers come with three start/select rubbers though, black, grey, and a transparent white. so many start/selects! I have more than I know what to do with now.

## face buttons
they're good, but face buttons are hard to get wrong.

the force to press them is comfortable, and they always register. not bad!

## verdict
they're pretty good pads, except for the L/R, which I can't really recommend. other than that, they should get you pretty far!
